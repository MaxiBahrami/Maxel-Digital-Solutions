DATA & PRIVACY

How the project brief handles your information.

A concise explanation of what happens when you use the static contact experience.

Your brief stays on your device

The project brief helper assembles the details you enter in your browser. It does not submit them to a server or save them in browser storage. Reloading or leaving the page clears the form, subject to your browser’s own form restoration behaviour.

Copying, downloading and email

Copying places the brief on your clipboard. Downloading saves a text file on your device. If an email contact is configured, “Open email draft” opens your chosen email app. You review and send that email yourself; this website does not send it.

External contact

The external contact link opens Maximilian’s personal website. Information you share there or through email is handled by those services.

Website services

This static website includes no account system, advertising trackers, analytics tools or contact database. Your hosting provider may retain standard access logs. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive records in a project brief.
